Commercial notes

Important rate conditions

Included in base hire

Base rates include car hire, fuel and chauffeur charges within the respective city municipal limit.

Charged extra

GST, interstate taxes, toll taxes and parking charges are charged extra as applicable and as per actuals.

Garage to garage

Time and mileage for spot-rental duties may be calculated from garage to garage.

Outstation minimum

Inter-city duties outside NCR may require a minimum of 300 km per day.

Cancellation and no-show

Cancellation terms may apply within 4 hours of reporting time. No-show can attract minimum local package charges.

Fuel changes

Rates may be revised if fuel prices change materially before confirmation or during long contracts.
